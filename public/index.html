    STANDBY
    AUTOMATIC TOURNAMENT

    Before each pre-semifinal match: 60-second match betting. Before the first semifinal: one 3-minute champion window for all four remaining fighters, paid after the grand final. No further match betting. Between championship matches: tree / character screens alternate every 8 seconds. Pausing does not cancel betting. Restarting never resumes games automatically.

    VIRTUAL-CREDIT CHAT BETTING / ALL CHANNELS

    Viewers start with 1,000 credits. Type !bet 100 <number>, !bet all <number> or !balance in chat. Choices 1–2 for match betting; 1–4 for champion betting. One final bet per window. Winners split the whole pool in proportion to their stakes; displayed payouts include returned stakes. Balances below 100 refill to 100 before an accepted bet. No money, purchases, cash value, prizes, redemption or Twitch Channel Points.

    Sign in with a separate Twitch bot account, never your streaming account. Grant chat:read and chat:edit. The bot reads wagers and posts rate-limited confirmations, balances and results in your configured stream channel. A lost connection during open betting refunds all stakes and pauses launches. Reauthorize after server restart. If there are no winning stakes or no opposing stakes, all stakes are refunded instead of paid out.

    CURRENT CREDIT BETTING WINDOW

    Set AUTOMUGEN_TWITCH_CLIENT_ID and AUTOMUGEN_TWITCH_CHAT_CHANNEL (your streaming channel's login) before starting. A separate bot account is required; streaming-account sign-in is rejected. Register http://localhost:8765/auth/callback as the OAuth redirect. Channel Points support has been removed. Any old unresolved native prediction must be handled manually in Twitch Creator Dashboard before acknowledging it here; this app cannot refund it.
